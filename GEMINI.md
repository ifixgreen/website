# AI Assistant Guidelines for iFix Green Solutions Website

## 🚨 MANDATORY WORK LOG RULE
Whenever you perform ANY task, fix, feature, modification, or configuration change in this repository or its associated services:

1. **Always update `README.md`**:
   - Locate the `## 📜 Complete Work & Change Log` section in `README.md`.
   - Add an entry under today's date summarizing the exact changes, files modified, or external infrastructure updated.
   - Keep entries organized in reverse chronological order (newest dates on top).

2. **Commit and Push**:
   - Ensure the updated `README.md` is committed along with your changes and pushed to GitHub (`main` branch) so that the log remains current on both local and remote repositories.
