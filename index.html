The Process

How Solar Energy Works

From sunlight to powering your home — a simple, efficient four-step process.

01

Solar Energy Capture

Photovoltaic panels capture sunlight and convert it into direct current (DC) electricity, ensuring optimal absorption.

02

DC to AC Conversion

Inverters transform direct current into alternating current for household and business use, ensuring efficient power conversion.

03

Powering Appliances

Converted electricity powers appliances, lighting, and equipment, reducing dependency on the main grid.

04

Energy Storage & Grid

Excess energy is stored in batteries or fed into the grid, ensuring uninterrupted power supply and sustainability.
